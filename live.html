Living in 62278: the honest relocation guide

Housing, money, schools, healthcare and commute — what life in Red Bud and the surrounding countryside actually looks like.

The short version

Red Bud is a city of about 3,800 people (2020 census) at the center of a wider 62278 ZIP area of roughly 6,400 residents. It sits where Illinois Routes 3, 154 and 159 meet, inside commuting range of St. Louis, with a local hospital, a community-college campus, parks, a pool and a public golf course.

The trade is simple: more house and quieter streets for less money, in exchange for a longer drive to big-city jobs, airports and nightlife. If that trade suits you, 62278 is one of the better-value corners of the Metro-East.

Housing

Redfin put the 62278 median sale price at about $200K in November 2025, up about 26% year over year, with homes selling in roughly 43–46 days. Census ACS 2023 estimates put the median home value near $181,500. Translation: prices are rising fast from a low base, and well-priced homes don't sit long.

  • Stock: older brick homes near downtown, newer subdivisions on the edges, and rural acreage across the county.
  • Rentals: limited — start early, and consider Waterloo or Columbia as backups.
  • Tip: get pre-approved before touring; competitive listings often go to the first clean offer.

Money

The ACS 2023 median household income for the ZIP area is about $85,017. With lower housing costs than most metro suburbs, that income stretches further — but Illinois property taxes are real, so budget them explicitly (see the mortgage calculator).

Healthcare, schools & services

  • Red Bud Regional Hospital serves the town; larger specialty systems are in the St. Louis area.
  • Public schools are run by the local district; Southwestern Illinois College (SWIC) operates a Red Bud campus with manufacturing and healthcare programs.
  • Red Bud Public Library, city pool, splash pad, parks and the North County Country Club golf course cover everyday recreation.

Commute

Downtown St. Louis is about 40 driving miles away. Plan for 50–60 minutes each way depending on traffic and bridge. Use the commute calculator to price it honestly — a daily commute can cost several thousand dollars a year in fuel alone.
