Events & traditions in 62278

A year in Red Bud: festivals, holiday walks and community nights. Dates shift each year — confirm with organisers before you go.

February

Chocolate Walk

Downtown shops and sweets — a winter favourite for couples and friends.

Spring

Redbud bloom season

The town takes its name from the native redbud tree, which blooms in spring — the best time for photos (and our photo contest).

Summer

Red Bud Firemen's Picnic

A long-running community tradition with food, rides, games and music — the 97th edition was held recently.

Summer

Parks, pool & concerts

Concerts, markets and family events in Ratz Park and Lincoln Park; the city pool and splash pad keep kids busy.

September

Hops and Hogs Brew-B-Que

Craft beer and barbecue in town — check the regional visitors guide for the date.

October

Witches Night Out

A Halloween-season shopping night downtown.

Late November

Lighted Parade & Tree Lighting

The official start of the holiday season.

December

Red Bud Christmas Walk

Christmas market, shopping bingo and local tastings across downtown.
