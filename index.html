FAQ

Quick answers

Why is the site called 62287 / OCCUR?

On a standard phone keypad, 6-2-2-8-7 spells O-C-C-U-R. We help events occur — planned well, booked on time and kept on budget.

Is the quote service really free?

Yes. Vendors may pay a referral fee; you pay nothing and are never obligated to book.

How far ahead should I book vendors?

For large weddings and galas, book the venue 10–12 months out and photographers, caterers and bands 8–10 months out. Small parties can come together in 6–10 weeks. Use the countdown checklist for exact dates.

How much of my budget should go to the venue and food?

For most weddings and galas, venue plus catering and bar typically takes 50–60% of the total. Our budget planner shows a full split by event type.

I'm a vendor. How do I get listed?

Basic listings are free — see the vendor directory. Featured listings and lead partnerships are on the advertise page.
