FAQ

Quick answers

What is 62278?

62278 is the ZIP Code for Red Bud, Illinois, in the northwest corner of Randolph County, about 36 miles southeast of St. Louis, Missouri. This site is an independent guide to that area.

Is this the official city website?

No. 62278.com is an independent publication. For permits, utilities and official notices use the City of Red Bud's own website and offices.

How much do homes cost in 62278?

Redfin reported a median sale price of about $200K in November 2025, up roughly 26% year over year. Small markets swing month to month, so ask a local agent for current comps.

Is the matching service really free?

Yes. Partners may pay us a referral fee; you pay nothing and are never obligated to hire anyone.
