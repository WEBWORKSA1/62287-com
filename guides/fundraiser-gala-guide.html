How to Plan a Fundraising Gala That Raises More Than It Costs

By the 62287.com editorial team · Updated 2026-11-26

A gala's success is net revenue, not attendance. Design every element around that number.

Set the goal first

Work backward from what the cause needs. Many organisations aim to keep costs well below a third of gross revenue; know your own target before you book anything.

Sponsors pay for the event

Offer tiered sponsorships — presenting, table, bar, dessert, auction — with clear benefits: logos, tables, speaking moments and social mentions. Ideally, sponsorships cover the hard costs so ticket sales and giving go to the mission.

Price tickets and tables

Price above cost per guest. Tables of 8–10 sold to companies are easier than individual tickets.

Design the giving moments

  • Paddle raise: one compelling story, one concrete goal, gift levels called from highest to lowest.
  • Auction: fewer, better items. Experiences outperform objects.
  • Raffle or games: check local rules on raffles and gaming licences first.

Control costs

Seek in-kind donations for venue, wine, flowers and printing. Keep the programme tight — under 90 minutes of speaking.

Follow up within 48 hours

Thank donors personally, share the total raised and show what it will fund.
