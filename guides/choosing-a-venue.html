Choosing an Event Venue: 15 Questions to Ask Before You Sign

By the 62287.com editorial team · Updated 2026-11-26

The venue sets your date, your capacity, your style and a big share of your budget. Ask these before signing.

Capacity & layout

  1. What is the seated capacity for my layout (banquet, cocktail, theatre)? Banquet seating needs roughly 10–12 sq ft per guest.
  2. Where do the dance floor, stage, bar and buffet go — and does capacity drop with them?
  3. Is there a separate space for a ceremony or breakout sessions?

Money

  1. Is there a food-and-beverage minimum, and what counts toward it?
  2. What are the service charge, gratuity and tax rates?
  3. Which items are included — tables, chairs, linens, AV, staff?
  4. What's the deposit and payment schedule? What happens if I cancel or postpone?

Rules

  1. Must I use in-house or approved caterers and bars?
  2. Are there noise curfews, décor restrictions (candles, confetti) or outside-vendor fees?
  3. Is event insurance required?

Timing & logistics

  1. When can vendors load in, and when must everything be out?
  2. What does overtime cost per hour?
  3. Is there parking, accessibility and a place for vendors to stage?

Risk

  1. What's the rain or heat plan for outdoor spaces?
  2. Who is my on-site contact on the day?
