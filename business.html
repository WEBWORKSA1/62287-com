Build your business in 62278

Ready-to-build lots, Foreign Trade Zone access, river, rail and highway logistics — and a workforce pipeline between two university systems.

The location pitch, in plain numbers

  • Red Bud Business Park: 50.58 acres bought by the city in 2017, planned as light-industrial/commercial lots of roughly 1–4 acres, with gas, water, sewer and fiber internet.
  • Foreign Trade Zone 31: the city sits inside FTZ 31, which can defer or reduce duties for qualifying import/export operations.
  • Roads: IL-3, 154 and 159 cross in town; the city cites reach to about 80% of the U.S. population within a two-day truck drive.
  • River: the Kaskaskia Regional Port District's Dock #2 is about a five-minute drive, giving barge access to the Mississippi system.
  • Talent: positioned between SIU Edwardsville and SIU Carbondale, with SWIC's Red Bud campus training manufacturing and healthcare workers.
  • Incentives: the city advertises loan programs, sales-tax rebate incentives and an enterprise zone; state programs stack on top. Eligibility varies — confirm with the city.

Sources: City of Red Bud economic development pages and Red Bud Business Park profile guide. Verify current lot availability directly with the city.

Who it fits

Light manufacturing, fabrication, ag-processing, distribution serving the St. Louis metro, contractors' yards, and service businesses priced out of Metro-East suburbs. It is a weaker fit for operations needing a large on-site labor pool from day one or daily air freight.
