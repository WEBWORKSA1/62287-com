🏆 Contests & prizes

Redbud Season Photo Contest

Capture spring in the 62278 area — redbud blooms, Main Street, farms, festivals. Winners are featured sitewide and receive sponsor-funded prizes (local gift cards and featured placement). Current prize details are posted here once each season's sponsors confirm.

Best Small Business Pitch

Pitch a business idea for Red Bud's downtown or business park in 300 words. Finalists get a free Featured listing for a year and a mentoring call.

Official rules (summary)

  • No purchase necessary. Void where prohibited.
  • Open to legal U.S. residents 18+. One entry per person per contest.
  • Entries must be your own original work; you keep copyright and grant us a licence to display your entry.
  • Winners chosen by a judging panel on originality, quality and relevance; notified by email within 14 days of close.
  • Prizes are non-transferable; sponsors are listed with each contest.
