🏆 Contests & prizes

Best Event Photo of the Season

Share your best shot from a wedding, party or event you hosted or attended (with the subjects' permission). Winners are featured sitewide and receive sponsor-funded prizes; current prize details are posted here once each season's sponsors confirm.

Dream Event on a Budget

Describe how you'd throw a memorable event for 50 guests on $2,500 — in 300 words. The most creative, realistic plan wins a featured article and a sponsor prize.

Official rules (summary)

  • No purchase necessary. Void where prohibited.
  • Open to legal residents of the U.S. and Canada aged 18+ (Québec residents excluded where required by law). One entry per person per contest.
  • Entries must be your own work; you keep copyright and grant us a licence to display your entry. Photos must have the subjects' consent.
  • Winners chosen by a judging panel on originality, quality and relevance; Canadian winners must answer a skill-testing question.
  • Winners notified by email within 14 days of close. Prizes are non-transferable; sponsors listed with each contest.
