About 62287.com

OCCUR — an independent, numbers-first hub for planning events.

Why “OCCUR”?

Type 6-2-2-8-7 on a phone keypad and it spells O-C-C-U-R. Our job is to help events occur: planned clearly, booked on time and delivered on budget.

What we do

We publish free planning calculators, practical guides, a vendor directory and a seasonal planning calendar, and we connect planners with vetted vendors when they're ready to book.

How we make money

Display advertising (Google AdSense), featured vendor listings, sponsorships and referral fees from vendors. Editorial content is never paid for unless clearly labelled as sponsored.

Our numbers

Budget splits, per-guest figures and space ratios are widely used industry rules of thumb, presented as planning estimates. Real prices vary by city, season and vendor. Spot something off? Tell us.
