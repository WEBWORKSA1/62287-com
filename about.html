About 62278.com

An independent, numbers-first guide to ZIP 62278 and the countryside southeast of St. Louis.

What we do

62278.com helps people decide whether to live, buy or build in the 62278 area — Red Bud, Illinois and its surroundings — and connects them with local professionals when they're ready. We publish practical guides, free calculators, a local directory and an events calendar.

Who we are

An independent digital publisher. We are not a government body and have no affiliation with the City of Red Bud, Randolph County, the State of Illinois or the U.S. Postal Service.

Data & methodology

  • Population, income, home value: U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey 2023 5-year estimates for ZCTA 62278 (via Population Review); 2020 census for the city of Red Bud.
  • Home sale prices & days on market: Redfin 62278 housing market page (November 2025).
  • Business park, FTZ, logistics, incentives: City of Red Bud economic development materials.
  • Cost-of-living index: an estimate used for directional comparisons only.

We refresh figures periodically. Spot something outdated? Tell us.

How we make money

Display advertising (Google AdSense), sponsorships, featured listings and referral fees from partners. Editorial content is never paid for unless clearly labelled as sponsored.
